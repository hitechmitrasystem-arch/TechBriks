FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Feel free to reach out to our team.

What is your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A typical web application takes 3-6 months, while mobile apps range from 4-8 months. We provide detailed timeline estimates during our initial consultation after understanding your requirements.

Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes, we offer comprehensive support and maintenance packages. Our services include 24/7 monitoring, regular updates, security patches, performance optimization, and dedicated technical support to ensure your systems run smoothly.

What industries do you specialize in?

We work across various industries including fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, SaaS, manufacturing, and logistics. Our diverse experience allows us to bring best practices from multiple sectors to solve your unique business challenges.

How do you ensure project quality?

Quality is built into every stage of our process. We follow agile methodologies with regular sprints and reviews, implement automated testing (unit, integration, E2E), conduct code reviews, and perform thorough QA testing before any release.

What is your pricing model?

We offer flexible pricing models including fixed-price projects for well-defined scopes, time and materials for evolving requirements, and dedicated team arrangements for long-term engagements. We'll recommend the best model based on your project needs.
